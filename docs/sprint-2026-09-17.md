# Sprint — first document delivered

**Thursday 17 September to Friday 9 October 2026.** Sixteen working days.

**Goal:** one real customer document goes through 508This and comes back
conformant, with an Accessibility Conformance Report, reviewed by a person.

Nothing in this file is a commitment until the Chairman makes it one.
Decisions go in `docs/leadership-standup.md`; this file tracks the work and is
updated as items land.

---

## Definition of done

"Delivered" is easy to claim and hard to mean, so it is pinned here. All of
these, or the sprint has not met its goal:

1. **A Word document from a real team** – not a fixture – has been uploaded,
   checked, remediated and returned to them.
2. **Every fix passed through a person** in the review queue. Nothing reached
   the customer that only a machine had looked at.
3. **The returned package is the remediated .docx, a tagged PDF export, and an
   ACR** in the VPAT 2.5 Section 508 layout, generated from the same
   assessment functions the job page uses.
4. **The document's own conformance is re-checked after remediation** by
   running detection on the output, and the report shows no open findings on
   any criterion it owes.

---

## Why Word first, and why a person

The fastest route to a delivered document is the format that can be fixed in
code. A .docx is XML: alternative text, heading levels, table header rows,
document language and colour are all readable and rewritable, and Word exports
a tagged PDF from a well-structured document for free. PDF intake is the
hardest version of the product – an untagged PDF has no structure to fix, only
to rebuild – and it comes after the pipeline exists, not before.

The review queue is not a stopgap. Alternative text a model drafts is a
proposal; a wrong one is a finding, not a fix, and the service's promise is
conformance. A person confirms each fix from day one, and the automation
earns trust by how rarely they have to edit it.

## The work, in order

### Week 1 (17–25 September) – the pipeline exists end to end

1. ~~**Retention decision** from the Chairman, so storage can be built.~~
   **Decided 21 September**, in `docs/leadership-standup.md`: deletion seven
   days after download, the job record scrubbed of its quotations at
   delivery, one figure at a time to a vision model under zero data
   retention, and v1 accepts CUI. That last part was decided against the
   recommendation and adds accounts and audit logging to this sprint – a
   reviewer typing their name is not authentication, and 800-171 requires
   authentication.
2. ~~**Remediation of the deterministic findings.**~~ Landed 17 September, with
   contrast as well. Each fix is a pure function from XML to XML in
   `src/domain/remediate.ts`, tested the same way detection is, and the output
   is re-detected before anything is marked fixed.
3. **Proposed alternative text.** **Unblocked 21 September.** A pass-through
   endpoint sends *one image* to a vision model and returns a draft. The same
   shape as Loadsy's detect endpoint: nothing stored, nothing logged, the key
   off the client. Never for a document the customer marks CUI. The test case
   is the 12-page federal submission with 76 undescribed figures that the
   triage turned up – a day of a reviewer's time against well under a dollar
   of model time.
4. ~~**The review queue.**~~ Landed 18 September, with the reviewer writing
   alt text and link wording rather than editing a draft. Proposals come with
   the vision-model pass, after the retention decision.

### Week 2 (28 September – 2 October) – delivery

5. **Re-detection on the output**, so the report describes what is delivered.
6. **The ACR.** Generate the VPAT 2.5 Section 508 edition as a Word document
   from `assessAll`; the Remarks column is `describeAssessment`.
7. **Tagged PDF export** – LibreOffice headless in the first instance; the
   Chairman decides whether that is good enough or whether Word on a Mac is
   the export path for v1.
8. **Download the package**: fixed .docx, PDF, ACR.

### Week 3 (5–9 October) – a customer

9. **Ask one team** for one document that needs an ACR. Josh's network is
   federal contracting; this is a conversation, not a campaign.
10. **Run it through**, with Josh as the reviewer. Fix what breaks. Deliver.
11. **Write down what the customer said**, in the standup log, as the first
    entry the product has that did not come from the team.

## Deliberately not in this sprint

PowerPoint, HTML, WCAG 2.1 or 2.2, accounts and sign-in, payment, a marketing
site beyond the landing page, and automatic PDF tagging. Each is real and each
would push the first delivered document out of reach.

**PDF intake came back in on 18 September,** when the Chairman sent real
contractor files and both were PDFs. Reading, checking and remediating them
all landed the same day. The reasoning for deferring it – an untagged PDF has
no structure to fix, only to rebuild – holds for *tagging*, which stays out,
and was wrong for detection and for the fixes that do have one right answer.
